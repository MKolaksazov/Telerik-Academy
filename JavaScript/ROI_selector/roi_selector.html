Hue & NDVI (from spectrum)

Draw a rectangle to read hue and decode NDVI from the color spectrum.
Mean / Std are computed on raw 8-bit values (0–255) via canvas.getImageData(). NDVI is decoded per pixel by matching its color against control points sampled from your device's own legend (magenta → red → yellow → green, fading to grey near 0), then averaged — not derived from the averaged color, since the decode is non-linear. Hue is shown alongside as a reference reading, using circular statistics since hue wraps at 360°. Greyscale / unmatched pixels (negative NDVI, for now, or background) are excluded and reported separately.
